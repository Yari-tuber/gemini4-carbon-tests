# FACTS & SOURCES

All numerical claims in *Every Indian Could Stand Inside One City* are verified from primary demographic, crowd-safety, and geographic sources:

1. **India Population (`> 1.4 billion` / `146 crore`)**:
   - UN DESA *World Population Prospects 2024* (2025 medium-variant estimate for India: `1,463.9 million` ≈ `146.4 crore`).
   - Source: https://population.un.org/wpp/
2. **World Population (`8.2 billion`, `5.6× India`)**:
   - UN DESA *World Population Prospects 2024* (2025 estimate: `8,231.6 million`; `8,231.6 / 1,463.9 = 5.62×`).
   - Source: https://population.un.org/wpp/
3. **Standing Concert Crowd Density (`2 people / m²` = `0.5 m²` per person)**:
   - Prof. G. Keith Still, *Introduction to Crowd Science* (CRC Press) & event safety guidelines: `2 persons/m²` (`0.5 m²/person`) is the standard safe upper density for a standing concert/public assembly before crowd flow becomes restricted.
4. **India Crowd Square (`27 km` wide, `732 km²`, `0.02%` of India's land)**:
   - `1,463,900,000 people ÷ 2 people/m² = 731,950,000 m² = 731.95 km² ≈ 732 km²`.
   - Side length: `sqrt(731.95) = 27.05 km ≈ 27 km`.
   - Share of land area (`732 ÷ 3,287,263 km² = 0.000223 ≈ 0.02%`).
5. **NCT of Delhi Area (`1,483 km²` → ~half filled: `49.4%`)**:
   - Census of India / *Economic Survey of Delhi*: National Capital Territory of Delhi total area = `1,483 km²`.
   - Fill ratio: `731.95 ÷ 1,483 = 49.36%` ("fills only half the city").
6. **World Crowd Square (`64 km` wide, `4,116 km²`)**:
   - `8,231,600,000 people ÷ 2 people/m² = 4,115,800,000 m² = 4,115.8 km² ≈ 4,116 km²`.
   - Side length: `sqrt(4,115.8) = 64.15 km ≈ 64 km`.
7. **Sikkim Area (`7,096 km²`, least populous Indian state, `58%` full / `42%` to spare) & Distance (`~1,118 km`)**:
   - Census of India (2011): Sikkim is India's least populous state (`610,577` residents) with an official area of `7,096 km²`.
   - Fill ratio: `4,115.8 ÷ 7,096 = 58.00%` (`42.0%` empty room to spare).
   - Great-circle distance from Delhi (`28.61°N, 77.21°E`) to Gangtok/Sikkim (`27.53°N, 88.51°E`): `~1,118 km` east.
