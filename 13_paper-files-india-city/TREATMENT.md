# TREATMENT — *Every Indian Could Stand Inside One City*

## 1. Three Candidate Structures & Choice

1. **Linear Zoom-Out Escalation**: Start from two people in a `1 m × 1 m` square → zoom out to `27 km` square → drop onto Delhi → zoom out to `64 km` square → drop onto Sikkim → end on orbital Earth. *Weakness*: Slow first 3 seconds; lacks an opening visual paradox and loses loop retention.
2. **Split-Screen Comparison**: Keep India's full map on the top half while stacking population squares on the bottom half. *Weakness*: Splits vertical `1080×1920` screen real estate, shrinking both the physical crowd pouring gag and the orbital blackout reveal.
3. **Seamless-Loop Scale-Cascade Briefing (Chosen)**: Open *in medias res* directly over the half-filled 3D basin of Delhi (`146 CRORE PEOPLE`), dive from orbit down to two concertgoers in a `1 m²` neon square, pull up into a `27 km` tray and **physically tilt-pour** the crowd into half of Delhi, extinguish all night lights across the rest of India, escalate to all `8.2 billion` humans (`64 km` square), fly `1,118 km` east along the Himalayas to pour them into Sikkim (`58% FULL`), and whip-glide back to the exact Frame-0 Delhi pose on *"tell them—"* so the ending sentence completes itself on the replay's first word. *Why chosen*: Instant 0-second visual hook, visceral 3D tray-pouring native move, dramatic orbital blackout contrast, and 100% seamless loop architecture.

## 2. Logline & Arc

- **Logline**: Packed at standard standing-concert safety density (`2 people/m²`), all 1.46 billion people in India fill only half of Delhi—and all 8.2 billion people on Earth fit comfortably inside India's least populous state, Sikkim.
- **Setup (0.0s – 16.0s)**: Paradox hook on half-full Delhi → orbital night-lights scale of 1.4B+ people → micro `1 m × 1 m` concert rule (`2 people/m²`) → pull-back to `27 km` tray (`732 km²`) tilting and pouring crowd particles into half of Delhi (`1,483 km²`).
- **Turn (16.0s – 27.2s)**: Rest of India's night lights go completely dark (`Mumbai: 0, Kolkata: 0, Chennai: 0`); then global golden flight arcs bring all `8.2 billion` people on Earth together, expanding the square to `64 km` (`4,116 km²`)—now dwarfing Delhi's dashed outline.
- **Ending & Loop Bookend (27.2s – ~37.6s)**: The `64 km` square flies `1,118 km` east across the Himalayan foothills into Sikkim (`7,096 km²`), pours in to fill just `58%`, and rockets back west to Frame 0's exact camera over half-full Delhi on *"tell them—"*.

## 3. Benchmarks

- **Charles & Ray Eames — *Powers of Ten* (1977)** & **3D Physicalized Cartography Shorts**:
  - *What to learn*: Continuous logarithmic altitude transitions (`1 m` human scale ↔ `100 km` regional basin ↔ `6,000 km` orbital limb), tactile 3D container rims that turn abstract areas into physical vessels, high-contrast uppercase readouts.
  - *What not to take*: Proprietary map tiles, copyrighted music stems, watermarks, or national land-border disputes (use clean NASA GIBS public-domain imagery and Natural Earth coastlines + admin-1 Delhi/Sikkim basins only).
